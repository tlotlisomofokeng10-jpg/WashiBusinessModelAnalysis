Business Model Analysis

Exploring the four primary business strategies for the Washi ecosystem.

💡

Base Assumptions (Washi Pay Model)

Washi processes ALL payments in-app and pays partners every Friday. Gateway: Stitch (EFT ~1.5%) or Paystack (Cards ~2.9% + R1). Average car wash price is based on the 'Usual' Wash at R200 (Gateway fee ~R6.80). Firebase costs are ~R1.00 per active user/month.

01

The "Free Washes" Model

The Original Concept

The user pays a high monthly subscription fee and gets a set number of "free" car washes per month.

Customer PriceR349 / mofor 4 washes
Payment Cost~R11.80per sub charge
Server Cost~R1.00per month
Payment Flow: Washi processes the R349 sub in-app. On Friday, Washi pays the partner ~R120 (negotiated rate) per wash used. Gateway fee on R349 is ~R11.00.

Profitability (R200 'Usual' Wash)

Net sub revenue: ~R337.

  • 1-2 washes: Washi profits (R97 to R217/mo).
  • 3+ washes: Washi loses money (-R23+ per user).
  • To scale safely: You must charge ~R599/mo to ensure Washi remains profitable even if a user uses all 4 washes.

Advantages

  • High guaranteed upfront revenue (R349 per user).
  • Great for B2B fleet managers who want predictable monthly expenses.
  • "Breakage" is highly profitable if users wash less than allocated.

Disadvantages

⚠️ High financial risk
If a user washes 3 or more times a month, Washi takes a massive loss on that customer.
  • Very high barrier to entry for regular consumers. R349 is a lot to commit to upfront.
  • Hard to negotiate with car washes as Washi forces them to accept a severely discounted rate.
02

The "Upfront Discount" Model

The user pays a medium subscription fee to unlock a massive flat discount (e.g., 40% off) at any partner car wash.

Customer PriceR149 / mo
Payment Cost~R5.90per sub charge
Server Cost~R1.00per month
Payment Flow: User pays the discounted R120 via Washi App at the wash (40% off R200). Washi processes R120 (gateway fee ~R4.48) and pays the partner R120 on Friday.

Profitability (R200 'Usual' Wash)

Net sub revenue: ~R143. Partner takes the 40% discount hit (gets R120). Washi absorbs the ~R4.48 transaction fee per wash.

  • Profitable up to 31 washes/mo.
  • Partner loss: Partners lose R80 per wash, making this nearly impossible to scale.
  • To scale safely: Washi must subsidize the discount so partners don't take the hit. To afford this subsidy, Washi would need to charge ~R299/mo.

Advantages

  • Immediate gratification for the user (savings seen instantly).
  • Zero financial liability for Washi per wash. The R149 subscription is almost pure profit.

Disadvantages

🛑 Extremely difficult to sell
Car washes hate giving massive upfront discounts because it destroys daily cash flow.
  • You will struggle to convince premium car washes to accept 40% less money upfront just to be on the app.
03

Micro-Sub + Cashback

Current Prototype

The user pays a small monthly fee to earn 15% cashback into an in-app wallet every wash. Full price is paid at the till.

Customer PriceR69 / mo
Payment Cost~R3.53per sub charge
Server Cost~R1.00per month
Payment Flow: User pays R200 in-app. Cashback is 15% (R30). Using the 70/30 Split: Washi funds 70% (R21) and the Car Wash funds 30% (R9).
Car Wash Payout: R200 - R20 (Washi Commission) - R9 (Their Cashback share) = R171 paid on Friday.

Profitability (R200 'Usual' Wash)

Net sub revenue: ~R66. Washi Net per wash = Commission (R20) - Gateway (R6.80) - Washi Cashback Portion (R21).

  • Washi takes a -R7.80 loss per wash.
  • Breakeven: 8.4 washes. Washi only loses money if a user washes 9+ times a month (very safe).

Advantages

💡 Recommended
The most balanced and scalable model for South Africa.
  • Extremely easy to sell to users (R69 is the price of a coffee; it's a "no-brainer").
  • Easy to sell to car washes: Guaranteed volume, zero cash-handling, and a clean R135 payout every Friday.
  • Massive psychological "Lock-In": Users won't go to competitors with R100 in their Washi wallet.

Disadvantages

  • "Double Friction": User pays a sub *and* full price initially, marketing must explain wallet benefits clearly.
  • Super-User Risk: If a user washes 9 or more times a month, Washi will start losing money on them due to the 70/30 split burden.
04

"Freemium" Wallet Model

Starbucks Strategy

App and wallet are 100% free. Earn 5% cashback. Upgrade to "Washi Plus" for a monthly fee to earn 20% and VIP perks.

Customer PriceFree / R99Base / Plus
Payment CostR0 / ~R4.42Base / Plus
Server Cost~R1.00per month
Payment Flow: User pays R200 in-app. Free users (5% CB): Washi funds 100% of the R10 cashback. Plus users (20% CB = R40): Washi funds 70% (R28) and Car Wash funds 30% (R12).
Car Wash Payout (Free User): R200 - R20 (Comm) = R180 on Friday.
Car Wash Payout (Plus User): R200 - R20 (Comm) - R12 (Cashback share) = R168 on Friday.

Profitability (R200 'Usual' Wash)

  • Free Users (5% CB): Net = Commission (R20) - Gateway (R6.80) - Cashback (R10). Washi makes +R3.20 profit per wash.
  • Plus Users (20% CB): Net Sub: ~R95. Net per wash = Commission (R20) - Gateway (R6.80) - Cashback (R28) = -R14.80 loss per wash.
  • Breakeven (Plus): 6.4 washes. Washi loses money only if a Plus user washes 7+ times a month.

Advantages

🔥 Top Strategy
The absolute best strategy for rapid user growth.
  • Zero barrier to entry. Anyone can download and start earning.
  • Thousands of users can be onboarded quickly, giving leverage when negotiating with partners.
  • Highly effective upsell funnel (showing users potential earnings with Washi Plus).

Disadvantages

  • Slower path to initial revenue; supporting free users on servers before they upgrade.
  • Super-User Risk (Plus): Washi loses money if a Washi Plus user washes 7 or more times a month.
